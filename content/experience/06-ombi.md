# Ombi

**Founder** · Apr 2020 – Aug 2022

## Narrative

*Status: `draft` — needs Eric's pass.*

Ombi was a video digital twin for places — a way to feel what somewhere is
actually like before you go, on iOS and web. Eric founded it and owned the whole
arc: 0-to-1 product, strategy and roadmap, more than 250 customer interviews,
marketplace growth and hiring. It converted 15% of viewers into reservations or
directions, held a 50% weekly-to-monthly active ratio, and grew to 120
businesses, 50 creators and 1,200 videos.

## Bullets

- Grew SMB & creator community to 120 businesses, 50 creators, 1200 videos
- Owned 0-1 product development, strategy & roadmap, customer discovery (250+ interviews), competitive analysis, marketplace growth, quantitative analysis, prototype testing, recruiting
