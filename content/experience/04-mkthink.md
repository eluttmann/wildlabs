## Narrative

*Status: `draft` — needs Eric's pass.*

Institutions like the State of Hawaii and Stanford University sit on decades of
facilities and operations data they cannot practically use. Eric built the AI
product that makes it legible — including a retrieval system that reads PDF
drawings and spatial data, collapsing a campus operator workflow from 24 hours to
10 seconds and returning up to 8% of billable hours. He set product vision and
strategy with the CEO, and led a team spanning software engineering, machine
learning, 3D modelling, data science, design and go-to-market.
