## Narrative

*Status: `draft` — needs Eric's pass.*

World Lens is a physical AI risk simulation product that models what an
emergency actually costs — response time, impact, and the cascading effects
across a built environment. Eric led product and design, shaping the data
strategy behind a multi-agent reinforcement learning model that simulates
humans, buildings, vehicles and climate together, and built the design
partnerships that positioned the product for data centre development.
