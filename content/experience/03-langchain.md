## Narrative

*Status: `draft` — needs Eric's pass.*

LangChain is the AI developer platform on the Forbes AI 50, used by 100,000
companies with 487 million downloads. Eric designed LangGraph Studio, the agent
development environment that lets enterprise teams see and build complex agent
workflows rather than debug them blind — now used inside Apple, Visa, Uber,
Replit and LinkedIn. The work supported the public launch of LangGraph Platform
during a period when the company grew from $7M to more than $10M in annual
recurring revenue.
